## Questions

### I. Fill in the Blanks

1. The distance between the tip of the thumb and the tip of the little finger when stretched is called a _______.
2. The distance from the elbow to the tip of the middle finger is called a _______.
3. Measuring distance using the length of one's foot is called a _______.
4. The distance covered in one normal walking step is called a _______.
5. The standard unit of measuring length is the _______.
6. The length of smaller objects like pencils and erasers is measured in _______.
7. A cloth merchant uses a _______ to measure the length of cloth.

---

### II. Match the Following

Match the object to be measured with the most appropriate non-standard body unit:

| No. | Object to be Measured | Non-standard Unit |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Length of a classroom table | Pace |
| 2 | Length of a classroom floor | Cubit |
| 3 | Length of a flower garland | Hand span |
| 4 | Length of a textbook | Foot span |
| 5 | Distance across the school ground | Finger width |

### III. Multiple Choice Questions

   A. Finger width
   B. Hand span
   C. Pace
