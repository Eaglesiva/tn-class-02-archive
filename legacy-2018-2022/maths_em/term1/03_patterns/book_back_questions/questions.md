## Questions

### I. Fill in the Blanks

1. The sound pattern made by a moving ox cart is _______.
2. Dance forms like Kummi and Kolattam demonstrate patterns involving sound and _______ movements.
3. The basic rhythmic sound in Kummi dance is produced by _______.
4. In the Bhoomi-Vaanam game, when the teacher says 'Bhoomi', students should clap towards the _______.
5. When the teacher says 'Vaanam', students should clap towards the _______.
6. A zebra has a natural pattern of _______ and _______ stripes on its body.
7. Repeating shapes and colours in a regular sequence creates a _______ pattern.

---

### II. State True or False

1. Clapping hands and tapping tables at regular intervals create sound patterns.
2. In dance movements, body actions and rhythmic sounds cannot be combined into patterns.
3. The black stripes on a tiger's coat form a natural colour pattern.
4. Using only a single colour continuously without any sequence forms a colour pattern.
5. Striking a vessel 1 time and then 3 times alternately creates an auditory pattern.

---

### III. Respond to the Following Questions

1. Give two examples of sound patterns that you hear in daily life.
2. Name three traditional dance styles that involve rhythmic body movements.
3. Name three animals whose bodies have distinct natural colour patterns.
4. Create a repeating colour pattern using two colours of your choice:
   - Sequence: _______, _______, _______, _______
5. Briefly describe the rules of the Bhoomi-Vaanam game.
