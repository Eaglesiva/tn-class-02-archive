## Questions

### I. Fill in the Blanks

1. A dice is an example of a _______ shape.
2. A matchbox and a textbook are examples of a _______ shape.
3. Marbles and footballs are examples of a _______ shape.
4. A birthday cap and a spinning top are examples of a _______ shape.
5. A water pipe and a piece of sugarcane are examples of a _______ shape.
6. When an eraser is pressed on an ink pad and stamped on paper, the 2D shape obtained is a _______.
7. Tracing the outline of a coin using a pencil gives a _______ shape.
8. Rotating a rectangular paper strip pasted on a stick rapidly produces a 3D _______ shape.
