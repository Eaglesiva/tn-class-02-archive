## Questions

### I. Fill in the Blanks

1. Arranging objects or possibilities in an organized, orderly sequence is called a _______ list.
2. If Arun has 3 alternative routes to reach school, he can choose among _______ different ways.
3. With 2 shirts and 3 pairs of pants, a person can make _______ different clothing combinations.
4. Distributing 10 flowers into two pots can be arranged in combinations like 10 + 0, 9 + 1, and _______ other ways.
5. When collecting data in a toy shop, toys are grouped based on their _______.
