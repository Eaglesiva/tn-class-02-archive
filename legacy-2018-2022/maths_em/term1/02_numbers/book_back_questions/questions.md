## Questions

### I. Predecessor, Successor, and In-Between Numbers

1. Write the predecessor (number that comes before):
   - a) _______ 25
   - b) _______ 40
   - c) _______ 78
   - d) _______ 90
   - e) _______ 15

2. Write the successor (number that comes after):
   - a) 37 _______
   - b) 49 _______
   - c) 62 _______
   - d) 89 _______
   - e) 99 _______

3. Write the in-between number:
   - a) 25 _______ 27
   - b) 49 _______ 51
   - c) 83 _______ 85
   - d) 98 _______ 100
   - e) 66 _______ 68

---

### II. Place Value Table and Expanded Form

1. Split the following numbers into tens and ones and write their expanded form:

| Number | Tens | Ones | Expanded Form |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 24 | _______ tens | _______ ones | _______ + _______ |
| 47 | _______ tens | _______ ones | _______ + _______ |
| 60 | _______ tens | _______ ones | _______ + _______ |
| 85 | _______ tens | _______ ones | _______ + _______ |
| 93 | _______ tens | _______ ones | _______ + _______ |

2. Answer the following place value questions:
   - a) Write all two-digit numbers having 7 in the ones place: _______
   - b) Write all two-digit numbers having 6 in the tens place: _______
   - c) Write all two-digit numbers having 8 in the ones place: _______

---

### III. Comparing Numbers and Number Names

1. Insert the correct comparison symbol (<, >, =):
   - a) 45 _______ 54
   - b) 78 _______ 78
   - c) 89 _______ 98
   - d) 63 _______ 36
   - e) 50 _______ 49

2. Choose the correct answer:
   - a) Which of the following numbers is smaller than 75?
      A. 71
      B. 85
      C. 98
      D. 79
   - b) Which of the following numbers is greater than 40?
      A. 25
      B. 49
      C. 39
      D. 18

3. Write the number names in words:
   - a) 25: _______
   - b) 40: _______
   - c) 68: _______
   - d) 73: _______
   - e) 99: _______

---

### IV. Addition Exercises and Word Problems

1. Add the following numbers:
   - a) 24 + 3 = _______
   - b) 14 + 5 = _______
   - c) 32 + 24 = _______
   - d) 45 + 23 = _______
   - e) 56 + 31 = _______
   - f) 62 + 25 = _______

2. Addition Word Problems:
   - a) Abhi plucked 24 guavas and Jessy plucked 33 guavas. How many guavas did both of them pluck altogether?
   - b) In an inter-school competition, a school won 30 gold medals and 21 silver medals. Find the total number of medals won by the school.
   - c) There are 15 boys and 24 girls in a classroom. Find the total number of students in the classroom.
   - d) In a bird sanctuary, there are 33 parrots and 15 peacocks. What is the total number of birds in the sanctuary?
   - e) Suji saw 12 children and 15 adults in a park. How many people did she see in all?

---

### V. Subtraction Exercises and Word Problems

1. Subtract the following numbers:
   - a) 38 - 5 = _______
   - b) 47 - 23 = _______
   - c) 56 - 30 = _______
   - d) 68 - 25 = _______
   - e) 98 - 25 = _______
   - f) 79 - 44 = _______

2. Subtraction Word Problems:
   - a) Kamali had 25 cucumbers. She gave 3 to her friends. How many cucumbers were left with Kamali?
   - b) A shop had 56 tender coconuts. If 30 of them were sold, how many tender coconuts were left in the shop?
   - c) Leela plucked 28 lemons. If she used 5 lemons to prepare juice, how many lemons remained?
   - d) Bala made 26 paper boats and Kala made 6 paper boats. How many more paper boats did Bala make than Kala?
   - e) There were 33 birds on a tree. If 11 birds flew away, how many birds were left on the tree?
   - f) A box contains 38 paint brushes. If 10 brushes are used, how many unused brushes are left?
