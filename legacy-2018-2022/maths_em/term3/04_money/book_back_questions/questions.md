## Questions

### I. Fill in the Blanks

1. The name of the currency used in India is the Indian _______.
2. The official symbol for the Indian Rupee is _______.
3. One ₹50 note is equal in value to _______ ten-rupee notes.
4. One ₹20 note is equal in value to _______ ten-rupee notes.
5. Two ₹5 coins together make _______ rupees.
6. Ten ₹1 coins together make _______ rupees.
7. One ₹100 note is equal in value to _______ fifty-rupee notes.

### III. Multiple Choice Questions

   B. ₹10
   C. ₹15
   D. ₹20

   A. ₹6
   B. ₹8
   C. ₹10
   D. ₹5

   A. ₹1
   B. ₹2
