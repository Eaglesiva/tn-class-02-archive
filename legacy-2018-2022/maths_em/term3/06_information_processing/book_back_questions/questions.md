### II. Pond Creatures Data Analysis

A survey of living creatures observed in a village pond yielded the following counts:
- Cranes: 8
- Ducks: 6
- Frogs: 5
- Turtles: 3
- Fishes: 12

Based on the data above, answer the following questions:

1. Which creature is found in the greatest number in the pond? _______
2. Which creature is found in the least number in the pond? _______
3. What is the total number of birds (cranes and ducks) seen at the pond? _______
4. How many more fishes are there than frogs? _______
5. What is the combined total of turtles and ducks? _______

### III. Students' Favourite Fruits (Pictograph Analysis)

(Each fruit symbol represents 2 students)

- Apple: 4 symbols (8 students)
- Banana: 6 symbols (12 students)
- Orange: 3 symbols (6 students)

Questions:

1. Which fruit is liked by the maximum number of students? _______
2. How many students like mangoes? _______
3. How many students like oranges? _______
4. How many more students like bananas than apples? _______
5. How many total students participated in this survey? _______
