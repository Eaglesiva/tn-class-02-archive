## Questions

### I. Fill in the Blanks

1. On a clock face, the short hand that indicates hours is called the _______ hand.
2. The long hand that indicates minutes is called the _______ hand.
3. When the minute hand points to 12 and the hour hand points to 4, the time is _______ o'clock.
4. There are _______ hours in one full day.
5. There are _______ minutes in one hour.
6. The minute hand takes _______ minutes to complete one full round of the clock face.

---

### II. Reading Clock Times

Write the time shown by each clock position:

1. Hour hand at 3, minute hand at 12: _______ o'clock
2. Hour hand at 7, minute hand at 12: _______ o'clock
3. Hour hand at 10, minute hand at 12: _______ o'clock
4. Hour hand at 1, minute hand at 12: _______ o'clock
5. Hour hand at 12, minute hand at 12: _______ o'clock

---

### III. Classifying Duration of Actions

Classify the following actions based on whether they take seconds, minutes, or hours to complete:

1. Blinking your eyes: _______
2. Brushing your teeth: _______
3. School working hours for a day: _______
4. Clapping your hands once: _______
5. Eating your lunch: _______
6. Sleeping through the night: _______

---

### IV. Short Response Questions

1. Name the two hands on a standard analog clock.
2. How many hours elapse between 9:00 AM in the morning and 1:00 PM in the afternoon?
3. Name three English calendar months that have 30 days.
