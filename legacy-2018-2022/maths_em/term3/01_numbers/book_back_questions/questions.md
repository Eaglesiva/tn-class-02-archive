## Questions

### I. Fill in the Blanks

1. Numbers that can be completely paired in twos without any remainder are called _______ numbers.
2. Numbers that leave one object remaining when paired in twos are called _______ numbers.
3. Any number ending with 0, 2, 4, 6, or 8 in its ones place is an _______ number.
4. Any number ending with 1, 3, 5, 7, or 9 in its ones place is an _______ number.
5. Numbers that denote the order or position of objects in a sequence are called _______ numbers.
6. In the expression 2 + 2 + 2 = 6, the number 2 is added _______ times.
7. Adding the same number repeatedly is called repeated _______.
8. If 3 is subtracted repeatedly from 15 until 0 is reached, it takes _______ steps.
