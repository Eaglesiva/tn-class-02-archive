## Questions

### I. Fill in the Blanks

1. A shape whose starting point and ending point meet together is called a _______ shape.
2. A shape whose starting point and ending point do not meet is called an _______ shape.
3. A square has _______ sides and _______ corners.
4. In a rectangle, the _______ sides are equal in length.
5. A triangle has _______ sides and _______ corners.
6. A circle has _______ sides and _______ corners (choose: no / four).
7. A straight line lying flat like a sleeping person is called a _______ line.
8. A straight line standing upright is called a _______ line.
9. A straight line that is leaning or sloped is called a _______ line.
10. A line that bends smoothly without sharp turns is called a _______ line.
