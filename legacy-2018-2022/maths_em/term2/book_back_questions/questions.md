## Questions

### I. Comparing Numbers and Finding Bigger / Smaller Numbers

1. Identify and write the bigger number from each pair:
   - a) 62, 26: _______
   - b) 56, 65: _______
   - c) 96, 69: _______
   - d) 73, 37: _______
   - e) 84, 48: _______

2. Find the smallest number among the following groups:
   - a) 50, 40, 18, 71: _______
   - b) 23, 65, 12, 49: _______
   - c) 95, 87, 78, 89: _______

3. Insert the correct comparison symbol (<, >, =):
   - a) 74 _______ 47
   - b) 58 _______ 85
   - c) 90 _______ 90
   - d) 63 _______ 36
   - e) 81 _______ 92

---

### II. Ascending and Descending Order

1. Arrange the following numbers in Ascending Order (smallest to biggest):
   - a) 25, 12, 45, 33: _______, _______, _______, _______
   - b) 67, 34, 89, 52: _______, _______, _______, _______
   - c) 91, 19, 54, 76: _______, _______, _______, _______

2. Arrange the following numbers in Descending Order (biggest to smallest):
   - a) 45, 78, 23, 62: _______, _______, _______, _______
   - b) 88, 33, 99, 55: _______, _______, _______, _______
   - c) 73, 54, 87, 17: _______, _______, _______, _______

---

### III. Number Names in Words

Write the number names in words for the following numbers:

1. 38: _______
2. 54: _______
3. 67: _______
4. 75: _______
5. 89: _______
6. 96: _______

---

### IV. Addition with Regrouping (Carry-Over)

1. Add the following two-digit numbers:
   - a) 28 + 15 = _______
   - b) 36 + 27 = _______
   - c) 45 + 38 = _______
   - d) 54 + 29 = _______
   - e) 63 + 18 = _______
   - f) 47 + 45 = _______

2. Addition Word Problems:
   - a) On one side of a field, Sujatha planted 24 mango saplings, and Babitha planted 36 mango saplings on the other side. How many mango saplings did they plant altogether?
   - b) Kayalvizhi collected 48 eggs and her friend collected 35 eggs from a poultry farm. How many eggs did they collect in total?
   - c) A library has 56 story books and 28 science books on a shelf. How many books are there in all?
   - d) A fruit vendor has 47 apples and 36 oranges in his cart. Find the total number of fruits in the cart.

---

### V. Subtraction with Regrouping (Borrowing)

1. Subtract the following numbers:
   - a) 42 - 18 = _______
   - b) 54 - 38 = _______
   - c) 63 - 27 = _______
   - d) 75 - 46 = _______
   - e) 81 - 35 = _______
   - f) 95 - 37 = _______

2. Subtraction Word Problems:
   - a) Thirumagan had 24 tamarind seeds. He used 16 seeds to play a game. How many seeds were left with him?
   - b) A car manufacturing company produced 95 cars in a day. If 37 cars were sold, how many unsold cars remained in the factory?
   - c) There were 62 flowers in a garden. In the evening, 28 flowers shed their petals. How many fresh flowers were left?
   - d) A bus was carrying 52 passengers. At a bus stop, 19 passengers got down. How many passengers were left inside the bus?
