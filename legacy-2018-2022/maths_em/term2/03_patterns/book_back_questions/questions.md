## Questions

### I. Fill in the Blanks

1. A pattern made by dipping thumbs in ink and stamping on paper is called a _______ print pattern.
2. Cutting a lady's finger crosswise and dipping it in paint creates a pattern resembling a _______.
3. Carving shapes into a potato and stamping them on paper creates a _______ print pattern.
4. Stamping fresh tree leaves with paint reveals the natural _______ of the leaf.
5. An arrangement of shapes, objects, or colours repeated in a regular, orderly sequence is called a _______.
6. An alternating sequence of a square and a triangle forms a geometric _______ pattern.

---

### II. State True or False

1. Vegetables can be used as printing blocks to create artistic repeating patterns.
2. Natural objects like fallen leaves and flower petals cannot form print patterns.
3. Thumb impressions can be used creatively to draw birds, animals, and insects.
4. A pattern has shapes arranged without any rules or regular order.
5. Folding coloured paper into triangles can be used to make a king's crown pattern.
